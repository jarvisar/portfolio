Hyperplay: 4D objects on a table, shown as 3D cross-sections.

About

A WebXR app for viewing and interacting with 4D objects and non-Euclidean space, built with Three.js for the Meta Quest with hand tracking. Controllers work too, and every scene also runs in a desktop browser with a mouse and keyboard, or touch.

Scenes

  • Hyperplay: a 4D physics sandbox where objects show up as 3D cross-sections.
  • Polytope Lab: the regular 4-polytopes, projected and sliced.
  • Knot Lab: a rope simulated in 4D, where knots come undone by moving strands through the fourth axis.
  • Hopf Garden: the Hopf fibration of the 3-sphere.
  • Hyperbolic Space and Spherical Space: honeycombs and the 120-cell, seen from inside.
  • Klein Room: walk through a wall and come back mirrored.
  • Quasicrystals: slices of 5D and 6D lattices that never repeat.
