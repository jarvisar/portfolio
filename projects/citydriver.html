About

A taxi driving game built with Three.js. Every visit generates a new island city with a harbor, a river, parks, and six districts, using a port of MapGenerator.

Pick up passengers and drop them off before the fare clock runs out, and spend the earnings on faster cabs. There's also a demolition mode with a truck and one minute to cause as much damage as possible. Free drive allows getting out to walk around, talk to people, or take a car from traffic.

Details

  • Traffic, pedestrians, weather, and night driving.
  • Keyboard, touch, game controller, and VR support.
  • Runs in the browser and as an Electron desktop app.
  • Version 2 of Citydriver, which streamed an endless grid city instead of generating an island.
