Sorting Algorithm Visualizer

Angular web app | 2023

Open Visualizer Source Android APK

An unsorted array.

About

An Angular app that animates 24 sorting algorithms, from insertion sort, merge sort, and heap sort to radix sort, bitonic sort, and stooge sort. The array size and speed can be changed, and each bar plays a tone based on its height as it moves.

Also available as an APK for Android.
