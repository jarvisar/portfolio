NASA Exoplanet Archive Search

Senior design capstone | 2022 - 2023

Open Exoplanet Search Source

A search for the TRAPPIST-1 system.

About

My senior design capstone at the University of Cincinnati, made with William Leithauser. It's a progressive web app built with Angular that queries NASA's Exoplanet Archive from a set of search fields and shows the results in a sortable, responsive table. It can be installed on most devices like a regular app.

Features

  • Search by host star, discovery method, year, and facility, with optional ranges for mass, radius, and density, star type, and the number of stars and planets in the system.
  • Expand a row for planet type, orbit details, and the planet's position in the sky. Terms in the expanded row link to searches for similar planets.
  • Configure columns, revisit previous searches, copy search parameters, and export results as CSV.
  • Results are cached, since large searches can take up to 15 seconds the first time.
  • Requests go through my CORS Proxy.
