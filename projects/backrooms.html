About

Inspired by the creepypasta "The Backrooms", an endless maze of empty yellow rooms. It started in 2023 as a simple Three.js maze using Prim's algorithm and has been expanded with new levels, game modes, and an editor since then.

There are two game modes. In Found Footage, the player collects eight notes and finds the exit while something follows, and each exit leads down to the next level. Explore lets you walk around any level with no goal, and has an edit mode for changing walls, lights, and furniture. The levels so far are Level 0, Level 1, Level 2 (Pipe Dreams), Level 4 (the Abandoned Office), Level 5 (the Terror Hotel), and Level 37 (the Poolrooms).

Details

  • The maze keeps generating as the player gets close to the edge. Settings for lighting, effects, and resolution keep it running on slower devices.
  • GLSL shaders add a CRT effect, vignette, and RGB color shifting. The shaders are largely from @felixturner.
  • Keyboard and mouse, game controller, touch, and VR support.
  • Playwright tests, including automated accessibility checks with axe-core.
