Coastline

Three.js game | 2025 - 2026

Play Coastline Source

Pacific Coast.

About

An endless driving game built with Three.js. Roads and scenery generate ahead of the car through nine routes, from coastal highways and a rainy downtown to snowy mountains, salt flats, and a cypress swamp. Each route has its own car, weather, and landmarks to find along the way.

All the terrain, models, and audio are generated in code, with no downloaded assets.

Details

  • A garage, several camera views, and autodrive.
  • Keyboard, touch, game controller, and browser VR support.
  • Also available as a desktop app built with Electron.
