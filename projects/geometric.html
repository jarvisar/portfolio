SVGeometric

Generative art | 2025 - 2026

Open SVGeometric Source

SVG artwork rendered from the generator.

About

I built this to make geometric line art for my pen plotter. Everything runs in the browser with no dependencies, and there are 49 designs, including spirographs, guilloche rosettes, flow fields, topographic contours, Truchet tiles, Islamic star patterns, Penrose tilings, and L-system fractals.

Pick a design and adjust its settings, or randomize it to find something new. Controls for paper size, margins, and pen width show how the plot will look before exporting it.

Plot-Ready Output

  • SVG export in millimeters, with a separate layer for each pen.
  • Everything is clipped to the page margins, and paths are joined and ordered to cut down pen movement.
  • Designs use a seed, so any variation can be recreated, saved as a recipe, or shared as a link.
