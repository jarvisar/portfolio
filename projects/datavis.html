About

Three projects from CS 5124, Visual Interfaces for Data, built with D3.js. Each one is a dashboard of linked charts: selecting a bar or label in one chart filters the others.

Projects

  • Out of This World: exoplanets from the NASA Exoplanet Archive by discovery method, star type, habitability, distance, and discovery year, with a mass and radius scatter plot. (Source)
  • Who You Gonna Call? 3-1-1: Cincinnati's non-emergency service requests on a Leaflet map, with a brushable timeline, response times, and calls by day, agency, and ZIP code. Made with Ryan Logsdon. (Source)
  • TV Time: transcripts from the first 11 seasons of The Simpsons, with lines by character and episode, a chord diagram of who talks to whom, frequent locations, and a word cloud. Made with Ryan Logsdon. (Source)
