About

A web app that turns any area of the map into a multicolor 3D printable model, sized for FDM printing with a 0.4 mm nozzle. It builds terrain, water, parks, roads, buildings, and trees from Overture Maps and public elevation data, and exports a Bambu Studio project with one filament per color.

Buildings can be measured from public LiDAR surveys where one covers the area, or the whole model can be built from the survey alone as one solid. Models can be rectangles, circles, or hexagons, can include GPX routes and racetrack laps in their own color, and can be colored by height or with one of twelve themes. Models larger than the print bed are split into plates. An editor in the 3D view changes buildings, roads, and land cover, and adds text and pins.

City Model is the web version of my earlier 3D Map Creator Blender add-on, with the same generation rules ported to TypeScript. The add-on was inspired by my hand-designed Jarvizar miniature maps.
