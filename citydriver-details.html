About

A taxi driving game built with Three.js. This is the second version of Citydriver. Instead of an endless grid, every visit generates a new island city with a harbor, a river, parks, and six districts, using a port of MapGenerator. Pick up passengers and drop them off before the fare clock runs out, then spend what you earn on faster cabs. There's also a demolition mode where you get a truck and one minute to cause as much damage as you can. In free drive you can get out and walk around, talk to people, or take a car from traffic. Supports keyboard, touch, controllers, and VR.
