About

A taxi driving game built with Three.js. Pick up passengers and drop them off before time runs out, earning tips for fast deliveries, drifts, and near misses. Money from fares can be spent on new taxis for your fleet. Free drive has no timer, with weather settings and city landmarks to discover. The city generates as you go, with traffic, waterfronts, and bridges throughout. Supports keyboard, touch, and controllers.
